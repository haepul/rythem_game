800×480 projection and stage aspect ratio retained. Top HUD ends above track top (110 logical px). Combo and judgment sit in the upper-right margin outside the track at their Y positions. Repeated animations are cancelled, cached elements reused, text written only when values change, displayed time updated once per second, progress quantized to 0.1%. No layout reads in HUD updates.
